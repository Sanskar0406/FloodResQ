Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries.

How can I report a flood incident? ▼
You can report incidents in real time using our "Report" page or by clicking the Emergency SOS button. Our AI system verifies water depth, geotags the risk, and alerts municipal rescue brigades immediately.
Can I volunteer with FloodResQ? ▼
Yes! We actively register boat operators, medical responders, food relief organizers, and local neighborhood captains. Click on "Volunteer" in the navigation bar to sign up.
How can my organization partner with you? ▼
Municipal corporations, hospital networks, NGOs, and corporate emergency units can connect their telemetry or dispatch feeds with FloodResQ. Submit the contact form above with subject "Partnership & Municipal Triage".
